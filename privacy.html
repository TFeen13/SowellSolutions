Privacy Policy

Sowell Solutions respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit our website, submit a contact or quote request, communicate with us by email or phone, or otherwise interact with Sowell Solutions online.

Information We Collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide through our website forms, email, phone calls, or other communications. This may include your name, email address, phone number, business name, website or social media page, business details, service interests, and any message or project information you choose to submit.

We may also collect limited technical and usage information automatically when you visit the website, such as browser type, device type, pages visited, referring pages, general location information, date and time of visits, and interactions with website content.

How We Use Information

We use collected information to respond to inquiries, review quote requests, provide business consulting services, communicate with prospective and current clients, improve our website, understand how visitors use the site, maintain security, and support business operations.

Forms and Communications

Quote and contact forms may be provided through Google Forms or similar tools. When you submit a form, the information you provide may be processed by that form provider and delivered to Sowell Solutions for review and follow-up.

Communications may be handled through Google Workspace, Google Voice, email, phone, or similar business communication tools.

Analytics and Website Tools

We may use analytics, performance, and website improvement tools such as Google Search Console, Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity, or similar services. These tools may help us understand website traffic, page performance, visitor interactions, search visibility, and how users navigate the site.

These services may use cookies, scripts, device identifiers, session recordings, heatmaps, or similar technologies to provide aggregated or diagnostic information about website use.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website and third-party tools may use cookies or similar technologies to support analytics, performance measurement, security, embedded forms, and basic site functionality. You can adjust cookie settings through your browser. Disabling cookies may affect how some website features work.

How We Share Information

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with service providers that help us operate the website, process forms, provide email or phone communications, measure website performance, or support business operations. These providers may include website hosting, Google services, Microsoft Clarity, analytics providers, and communication tools.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect our rights, to prevent fraud or abuse, or to respond to lawful requests from public authorities.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect information. However, no website, email system, form tool, or internet transmission is completely secure. Please avoid submitting highly sensitive information through the website unless specifically requested.

Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to inquiries, provide services, maintain business records, improve operations, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes.

California Privacy Rights

California residents may have certain privacy rights regarding personal information, including rights to know what information is collected, request deletion, correct certain information, and opt out of certain sharing or sale practices where applicable. Sowell Solutions does not sell personal information.

To make a privacy-related request, contact us using the information below.

Children’s Privacy

This website is intended for business use and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Third-Party Links and Embedded Services

This website may include links to third-party websites or embedded services, such as Google Forms. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or policies of third-party services.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
