Services

Practical Support for Businesses That Need Structure

Sowell Solutions helps businesses move from scattered operations to clear, repeatable systems. Whether the problem is unclear workflows, missing SOPs, inconsistent handoffs, or messy internal processes, each service is designed to bring order, accountability, and smoother execution.

Package One

Workflow Audit

A deep review of how the business currently operates, where work gets stuck, and which internal processes need the most attention.

  • Process review: Examine current workflows and daily operating habits.
  • Workflow mapping: Visualize how tasks move between people, departments, or systems.
  • Bottleneck identification: Find the delays, gaps, and repeat issues slowing the business down.
  • Team handoff review: Clarify where responsibilities transfer and where communication breaks down.
  • Written recommendations: Provide clear findings with practical next steps.

Package Two

SOP & Systems Buildout

For businesses that need documented procedures, cleaner internal structure, and repeatable systems that teams can actually follow.

  • Standard Operating Procedures: Create clear SOPs for recurring tasks and core workflows.
  • Process documentation: Turn verbal instructions and scattered knowledge into written systems.
  • Role and task clarity: Define who owns what and how each process should move.
  • Training-ready workflows: Build documentation that supports onboarding and consistency.
  • Internal checklists: Create practical tools teams can use during daily operations.

Package Three

Implementation Support

Hands-on support for putting the improvements into place, refining the process, and helping the business adopt cleaner ways of working.

  • Workflow improvements: Adjust processes so work moves more cleanly from start to finish.
  • Team process rollout: Help introduce new procedures in a clear and usable way.
  • Operational cleanup: Remove confusion, duplicate work, and unnecessary friction.
  • Efficiency improvements: Simplify tasks and reduce wasted time where possible.
  • Follow-up refinement: Review what is working and fine-tune the system after implementation.
